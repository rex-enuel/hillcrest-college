Logo Concepts

Concept 1: The Hill Crest

Monogram with triangle

Decision: Uses triangle as hill silhouette. The "HC" monogram centralizes the hill's peak - representing both achievement and the school's elevated location.

Why: Direct geographic reference + lettermark for versatility. Gold circle = achievement/success.

Concept 2: Inclusive Unity

Circular unity

Decision: Circle represents inclusivity and unity. Two figures holding hands show community. Green path suggests growth journey.

Why: Directly communicates special needs inclusion. Circular = no hierarchy, everyone connected.

Concept 3: Heritage Badge

Shield badge

Decision: Shield = protection, heritage. Triangle hat suggests school structure. Serif typography reinforces tradition.

Why: Military-like discipline meets educational protection. Feels established, trustworthy.

Concept 4: Rising Sun

Abstract symbolism

Decision: Sun rising over hills = new day, new opportunities. Simple, memorable, modern.

Why: Positive, forward-looking. Works well on dark backgrounds. Easy to recognize quickly.

Concept 5: Modern Hill

Geometric modern

Decision: Terracotta triangle = warmth. Simple, bold shapes. Modern sans-serif treatment.

Why: Clean, contemporary feel. Works for digital/social. Bold enough for signage.

Website Style Variations

Style 1: Traditional Heritage

Typography: Serif headings (Cormorant Garamond), clean sans (Outfit)

Layout: Structured columns, academic grid system

Motion: Subtle fades, slide-in content

Imagery: Formal school photos, hill panoramas

Rationale: Respects heritage while remaining accessible. Gold accents highlight important sections. Serif conveys authority. Works for parents and alumni.

Style 2: Modern Inclusive

Typography: Bold sans-serif (Outfit), clean hierarchy

Layout: Asymmetric, airy spacing, inclusive imagery

Motion: Smooth transitions, scroll-triggered reveals

Imagery: Students with diverse abilities, collaborative learning

Rationale: Highlights inclusion mission. Cyan/TEAL signals modern education. Clean layout feels welcoming. Appeals to progressive parents.

Style 3: STEM Future

Typography: Geometric sans-serif, tech-oriented

Layout: Grid-based, data visualizations

Motion: Micro-interactions, hover animations

Imagery: Robotics, coding, lab work, partnerships

Rationale: Connects to Imperial College partnership. Green suggests innovation and growth. Modern aesthetic attracts STEM-focused families. Positions school as forward-thinking.

Style 4: Warm Community

Typography: Friendly sans-serif, rounded elements

Layout: Card-based, conversation-ready

Motion: Gentle animations, bounce effects

Imagery: Smiling students, family moments, school events

Rationale: Terracotta warmth builds trust. Rounded corners feel approachable. Card layout organizes information clearly. Emphasizes community over institution.

Style 5: Minimalist Heritage

Typography: Single font family, strong hierarchy

Layout: Generous whitespace, focused content

Motion: Subtle only, emphasis on reading

Imagery: High-quality photos, ample negative space

Rationale: Timeless elegance. Reduces cognitive load. Premium feel without complexity. Works across devices. Gold accents draw attention without overwhelming.
