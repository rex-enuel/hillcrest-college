Our Journey

From a Mother's Cry to a Hilltop Beacon

Every milestone tells a story of love, inclusion, and unwavering commitment to every child.

1983 — The Beginning

A Mother's Cry for Help

The story of Hillcrest dates back to a mother's cry for help. A daughter lost to a brain tumour, a son diagnosed with autism. The family home called Hillcrest — named after the man-made hill in front of it — was opened to the public. Mothers were invited to bring their children with special needs alongside their normal brothers and sisters. The response was overwhelming.

1998 — Secondary Arm Born

Growth & Expansion

The birth of the secondary arm marked Hillcrest's evolution from a special needs school to a comprehensive educational institution. The inclusive philosophy remained at the core — every child, regardless of ability, learning together in the same classrooms, the same assemblies, the same community.

2013 — The Hilltop Move

Renamed Hillcrest College Calabar

The move to the permanent hilltop site on Lemna Road, Ikot Effanga Mkpa. Built majestically on a hill, the campus offers a serene, natural learning environment with 360° views. The original farmhouse was converted to administration offices. Multiple classroom blocks crown the hill — a physical manifestation of reaching new heights.

2024 — STEM Future

Imperial College London Partnership

July 2024: STEM outreach collaboration with Imperial College London. 35 attendees at workshops targeting girls for coding and renewable energy. This partnership represents Hillcrest's commitment to preparing students for a global future while staying rooted in the inclusive values that began on this hill four decades ago.
