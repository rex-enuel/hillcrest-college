## VISUAL POSITIONING ANALYSIS

### Color Psychology Application

**Gold (#d4af37):**
- Used for: Hero section, CTA buttons, sun elements
- Effect: Signals achievement, prestige, warmth
- Why: Connects to "gold medal" achievement culture in Nigerian schools

**Terracotta (#cc7357):**
- Used for: Timeline markers, background accents
- Effect: Earthy, traditional, grounded
- Why: Connects to African earth tones, sense of place

**Cream (#fef8f0):**
- Used for: Background, text areas
- Effect: Clean, light, inclusive
- Why: Provides breathing room, accessibility

**Accent Cyan (#4a9da8):**
- Used for: Portal features, interactive elements
- Effect: Trust, communication, growth
- Why: Modern, approachable, differentiates from competitors

**Growth Green (#6abf69):**
- Used for: STEM section, positive actions
- Effect: Future, innovation, health
- Why: Connects to STEM initiative, positive growth

**Brown (#8b4513):**
- Used for: Text, building silhouettes
- Effect: Stability, tradition, authority
- Why: Grounding, traditional Nigerian earth tone

## TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTIONS

### Headlines (Cormorant Garamond - Serif):
```
Why: Traditional authority, Nigerian academic prestige
Usage: H1, H2, section titles
Size: 2.5rem-4rem
```

### Body (Outfit - Sans):
```
Why: Modern accessibility, clean readability
Usage: Paragraphs, navigation, forms
Size: 1rem-1.5rem
```

### Accent (Outfit - Sans Bold):
```
Why: Emphasis, hierarchy
Usage: CTA buttons, key facts, statistics
```
